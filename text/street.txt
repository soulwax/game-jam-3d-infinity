# THE QUARANTINE — the street, §46's five-phase arc, days 1-21.
# Days 1-4: traffic, voices, a bus, a radio. Ordinary and unremarked.
# Days 5-9: traffic thins, voices become single voices, the dog (a recurring, unnamed dog
# heard barking, noticed only in retrospect once it stops appearing after day 9).
# Days 10-14: no engines; birds, noticed only because they can now be heard.
# Days 15-18: the birds stop; wind, the eaves, rain on glass.
# Days 19-21: nothing. Not silence — absence. Room tone that is the house's own.
# Nobody ever remarks on the pattern; only the content itself shifts.

@street 1
traffic on the main road, voices past the window, a bus grinding gears, a radio next door

@street 2
the same traffic, someone laughing at the corner, the late bus, a radio turned up loud

@street 3
cars along the row, voices by the gate, the bus on its hour, a wireless two doors down

@street 4
traffic down to occasional cars, one bus passing near-empty, a single voice, a faint radio

@street 5
a car now and then, one voice down the street, and a dog barking two gardens over

@street 6
barely a car all morning, someone's wireless faint through the wall, the dog again

@street 7
an occasional car, a single voice at the gate, the dog carrying from behind the row

@street 8
a car passing rarely, no radio today, the dog barking off toward the allotments

@street 9
one voice, then nothing, the dog barking late and further off than usual

@street 10
no engines at all today, a bird calling from the guttering

@street 11
birds in the yard at first light, nothing else moving

@street 12
birdsong along the row, no cars, no voices at all

@street 13
birds again at dawn, the street otherwise still

@street 14
birds calling through the morning, the house around them quiet

@street 15
no birdsong today, only wind picking up in the eaves

@street 16
wind in the eaves, a loose slate somewhere above

@street 17
wind against the glass, nothing else audible

@street 18
rain on the window, the eaves running, wind dropping toward dusk

@street 19
no wind, no rain, only the house settling into itself

@street 20
a quiet that isn't the street's, a board finding its own temperature

@street 21
the house's own tone, nothing arriving from outside it at all
