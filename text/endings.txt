# THE QUARANTINE — The three endings of Day 21.
# WP-B responsibility: author all three endings per §17.4 and WP-B brief.
# None is heroic; none explains the sun; synchronisation is the only revelation.
# No ending resolves §9's central question; each is reachable from distinct game states.

@ending compliance
The broadcast ends. The light moves through the window and is gone.
Nothing knocks at the door. You lock it. Upstairs, the landing is empty.
The bedroom door is closed. The bed is there. You lie down. You sleep.

@ending rupture
You open the kitchen door. The kitchen is there. You reach to turn on the light.
Your hand finds the wall in the wrong place. You pull back. The oven is also wrong.
You step toward it. The space does not hold. You step back. The room is still there and still wrong.

@ending synchronisation
You read the entries in order. The true ones are there: eleven windows counted and verified.
Three landings. The mark on the doorframe. The handwriting on the tins is yours and legible.
These fit. The false ones do not fit—a word in the wrong place, a detail that dissolves.
They are passed over. What was distributed across twenty-one days is now gathered.
Nobody will know if you were right. The page ends. The house is quiet.
