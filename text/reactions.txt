# Three threshold choices from the first narrative pass. These are deliberately
# ordinary actions with consequences that can be remembered later.

@reaction sibling 2 full 4 pears
@option take-in
Take the pears in before they bruise.
@reply
All right. I heard you move. Keep the soft one for yourself.
@effect denise.pears=taken
@option leave
Leave them on the step, where she put them.
@reply
That's fine. The birds will have them by morning, then. They always do.
@effect denise.pears=left
@option answer-through
Answer her without opening the door.
@reply
You sound like Dad when you do that. Not the words. The place you put them.
@effect denise.pears=answered

@reaction child 3 full 1 pencil
@option offer
Slide the pencil through the letterbox.
@reply
Thank you. Don't shut it yet. I want to hear the house think.
@effect sylvia.pencil=given
@option keep
Keep the pencil. Tell her you have none to spare.
@reply
That's all right. I can use the red one. It writes when it wants to.
@effect sylvia.pencil=kept
@option ask
Ask why she needs it before you answer.
@reply
For the numbers. They keep changing when I look away.
@effect sylvia.pencil=questioned

@reaction warden 5 full 3 record
@option whole
Show him the whole journal, open flat.
@reply
Thank you. That is more than the box requires. I won't read the private parts.
@effect sowerby.record=whole
@option card
Show him only the page with the collection card.
@reply
That will do. You have kept the useful distinction. Most people don't.
@effect sowerby.record=card
@option close
Close the book and say the record is personal.
@reply
Of course. Personal is a category. I'll write “not produced” and come again.
@effect sowerby.record=refused

@reaction sibling 14 full 2 revision
@option accept
Let Denise's version stand.
@reply
Thank you. I wanted you to say that, but not because I wanted to win.
@effect denise.revision=accepted
@option prosecute
Ask whether the rest of her memory is wrong too.
@reply
There it is. I give you one thing back and you weigh the whole house with it.
@effect denise.revision=prosecuted
@option pencil
Offer her the pencil and ask what she wants remembered.
@reply
No. Keep it. I have enough things that belong to me in other people's houses.
@effect denise.revision=offered

@reaction stranger 17 compressed 1 case
@option accept-case
Take the sewing case and ask what is inside.
@reply
The things she kept from the light. A button, a blue thread, and the name she used before yours.
@effect stranger.case=accepted
@option ask-source
Ask where they found it.
@reply
In a room that had not chosen to be locked. Your mother left doors between.
@effect stranger.case=questioned
@option leave-case
Leave the case on the step.
@reply
Good. Keep the seam where it is. Some houses only stay shut while nobody claims the key.
@effect stranger.case=refused

@reaction caller 5 full 3 dish
@option settle
Say the dish is here and settle the debt.
@reply
Then I'll call once, and no more. My mother would have called that decent.
@effect hallow.dish=returned
@option ask
Ask where the dish was last left before you look.
@reply
On the dresser by the back door. Blue rim, hairline crack. I know a thing by its damage.
@effect hallow.dish=asked
@option withhold
Say the previous tenant is not recorded here.
@reply
No. That's not the same as saying she didn't owe me.
@effect hallow.dish=withheld

# Material residues are revealed only when the player looks at an existing
# object. They are notices, not pickups; the ordinary inspection remains the
# fallback when the fact is absent.

@residue denise.pears=taken bread-loaf
The paper beneath the loaf holds a sticky crescent and the faint smell of pear.

@residue sylvia.pencil=given journal-desk
A fresh pencil shaving sits in the desk groove, though the sharpener is closed.

@residue sowerby.record=refused journal-desk
The page edge is pressed flat where another hand asked for it and was refused.

@residue stranger.case=accepted journal-desk
The sewing case rests beside the journal. Its brass clasp is warm, though the room is cold.

@residue hallow.dish=returned hall-mail
A narrow receipt lies with the post. The ink has bled at the word “dish,” but the amount is clear.
