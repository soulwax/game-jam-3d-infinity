# THE QUARANTINE — Board wireless broadcast, days 8-14.
# The liturgy is fixed: date, status, sighting, instructions, closing.
# Sectors stay the closed set week 1 established: 6, 9, 11.
# Week 2's spine: the city is collectively editing what it believes, and this is
# the instrument. It states, restates, strains, contradicts itself, and corrects —
# and the correction is always smoother than the thing it corrects.
# Population layer (wave 5): still no names. People are now plural nouns WITH BEHAVIOUR —
# repeating, enquiring, awaiting examination, failing to make a return. The document is
# visibly about a population that is not behaving as the document expects.
# LOCKED BY VISITOR QUOTATIONS — change these and you break another file:
#   day 9 status "settled, orderly and entirely as expected" — ayling.txt day 9 full.2 quotes
#     this verbatim, so the clause itself is fixed; the drift moved onto "across|over" instead
#     (was previously {as expected|as advised}, which broke the quote on the other reading).
#   day 9 instructions "four hours from the eleventh"  — gas-man.txt day 9 full.2 / compressed.2
#   day 12 instructions "on the ninth"  — postman.txt day 12 says the eighth, deliberately
#   day 13 instructions "nothing at sector 9 is outstanding" — ayling.txt day 13 full.1

# Day 8 — the first sighting that says something. Careful to the point of saying
# almost nothing, and short enough that a resident can repeat it back word for word.
# Mundane reading: someone walked the wrong side of the cordon and was turned back.
@day 8
@part date
Eighth day of the order.
@part status
Boundary holding at sector 6. All returns from 409 households {received|collected} on time.
@part sighting
A movement near the sector 6 line has been accounted for. Residents need not repeat it further.
@part instructions
Residents of the even numbers are reminded to keep front glazing shuttered after dark.
@part closing
This has been a message from the Board.
@cue no-effect

# Day 9 — the announcement is already settled. The status performs calm at a volume
# calm does not require, and the sighting exists only to close a thing twice.
@day 9
@part date
Ninth day of the order.
@part status
Conditions {across|over} sectors 6, 9 and 11 are settled, orderly and entirely as expected.
@part sighting
The matter at the sector 6 line remains accounted for. Enquiries are no longer being taken.
@part instructions
Households in receipt of the allowance are reminded that it stands at four hours from the eleventh.
@part closing
This has been a message from the Board.
@cue no-effect

# Day 10 — the pivot. The second statement does not sit against the eighth: a
# different sector, two movements where there was one, and an hour nobody gave
# before. Every detail is worth writing down and every one of them is doubtable.
# Mundane reading: two clerks, two sets of notes, one slot to read them in.
@day 10
@part date
Tenth day of the order.
@part status
Boundary holding. Returns from sector 9 are {delayed|deferred} pending {a second|a further} count.
@part sighting
Two movements at the sector 9 line {shortly after|shortly before} first light are accounted for.
@part instructions
Every household is asked to keep the records of its occupants {legible|orderly} and available.
@part closing
This has been a message from the Board.
@cue no-effect

# Day 11 — a small undeniable event, and the hours treated as long since settled.
# Nothing is explained and nothing is named. Mundane reading: a scheduled test of
# equipment that has been tested before, and rooms chosen for the heat.
@day 11
@part date
Eleventh day of the order.
@part status
Boundary holding. Conditions in the {forenoon|afternoon} hours are unchanged at sectors 9 and 11.
@part sighting
The signal was sounded across sectors 6, 9 and 11 at ten past six. The apparatus is in order.
@part instructions
Persons awaiting examination are instructed to keep to the north rooms during the settled hours.
@part closing
This has been a message from the Board.
@cue no-effect

# Day 12 — rumour has become fact. Revised ration hours are reported as announced on
# the ninth; the ninth announced no such thing. Stated twice, so it sounds remembered.
# Mundane reading: a bulletin the resident's set was not tuned for.
@day 12
@part date
Twelfth day of the order.
@part status
Boundary holding. Ration hours for households in sectors 9 and 11 stand as revised on the ninth.
@part sighting
Residents' accounts of the sector 6 movement now agree in every particular. The matter is closed.
@part instructions
Every household is reminded that the revised hours were {published|circulated} on the ninth.
@part closing
This has been the message from the Board.
@cue closing-degrades

# Day 13 — the status and the sighting cannot both be true. The instruction resolves
# it in favour of the status and does not mention that anything was resolved.
# Mundane reading: a correction reached the desk between one paragraph and the next.
@day 13
@part date

@part status

@part sighting
One further movement at the sector 9 line remains unaccounted for and is under {review|inquiry}.
@part instructions

@part closing

@cue no-effect

# Day 14 — the capstone. A harder warning that explains nothing, and a week that
# ends by declining to reconcile its own reports.
# Mundane reading: an overstretched office closing a file it cannot finish.
@day 14
@part date
Fourteenth day of the order.
@part status
The order continues into a third week. Boundary conditions are not as they were on the first day.
@part sighting
Reports from sectors 6, 9 and 11 do not agree. Eleven households have made no return.
@part instructions
Every household is required to remain within its {own walls|own rooms} until further notice.
@part closing
This has been a message from the Board. It will not be repeated.
@cue closing-degrades
