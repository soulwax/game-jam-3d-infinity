# THE QUARANTINE — Mrs Ayling, number 9. Days 1, 9, 13, 17, 18, 20.
# She is already canon: week-3.txt day 17 appoints her warden, in a document that had
# been agentless for sixteen days. §17.3's beat for day 17 is "a minor character
# becomes central", so for sixteen days she must be genuinely, unremarkably minor.
# She is not a plant and not a schemer. A player replaying should find nothing in days
# 1-13 that predicted this and everything in them that made it plausible.
# What happens to her is entirely ordinary and entirely human. That is why it is week 3.
# Branch encoding per the wave brief §2: full.N / compressed.N, one line per tier.
