# THE QUARANTINE — the Bell sisters (twins). Days 4, 11, 17.
# Identical twins who live together; only one is ever at the door at a time,
# never both. The player cannot reliably tell them apart, and neither can
# several of the neighbours. This puts the drift mechanic's own logic into a
# living person: a detail can be "wrong" — the wrong twin, the wrong day, the
# wrong thing said — in a way that is completely mundane, because people
# confuse twins constantly. Never framed as anything but that.
#
# Boring explanation, held through Day 14 and never confirmed: they are
# identical twins, they have always been mixed up, and even people who have
# known them for decades still get it wrong.
#
# Branch encoding per the wave brief: full.N / compressed.N, one line per tier.

@visitor twins

# --- Day 4 — the first appearance. Two of them, established clearly and
# warmly. Whichever sister is at the door introduces the situation almost
# apologetically. full.2 gives the one small checkable detail — a scar — that
# Day 17 will need to unsettle later.
@day 4
@arrival 17 0
@tier full.1

@tier full.2

@tier full.3

@tier full.4
