# THE QUARANTINE — Mr Voss, Board records clerk. Days 8, 10, 13.
# Where Mr Sowerby checks compliance in person, Voss is the paperwork behind him: he
# has never been in this house and has no way to know it beyond the compliance card
# and the ration book (PLAN §45). Younger and more literal than the warden. He treats
# the card as the whole truth because to him it is. Never sinister, never presses.
# Branch encoding per the wave brief: full.N / compressed.N, one line per tier.

# --- Day 8, his first visit. Entirely procedural: he audits the card against the
# Board's own master copy and finds a small mismatch he is required to note, not
# resolve. Boring explanation: two clerks, two copies, one of them behind by a day.
@visitor clerk
@day 8
@arrival 9 0
@tier full.1
Board records. I check the card against our master copy — nothing further today.
@cue thinning
@tier full.2
There's a mismatch on the fourth. I note it, I don't resolve it. Not my role, that.
@cue thinning
@tier full.3

@claim object the compliance card
@cue thinning
@tier full.4
I'm not here to say who's right. I'm here so it's on file. That's the whole visit.
@cue thinning
