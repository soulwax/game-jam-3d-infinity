# THE QUARANTINE — the doctor. Days 6, 11, 15.
#
# Dr Marchant, Board medical. Where the warden audits your compliance, he audits you,
# and PLAN §9's question makes that the most loaded examination in the game without one
# line acknowledging it. So it is played completely straight: competent, unhurried,
# kind in a professional register. Ordinary questions — sleep, appetite, headaches,
# memory. He never diagnoses and never reacts to an answer. He writes it down, and the
# writing-down is the whole beat: the player is being recorded by someone else's
# instrument while their own is failing. He never confirms or denies anything about
# their state, and §9 is not resolved out loud by the one man qualified to resolve it.
#
# Boring explanation, held for days 6-14 and never confirmed: a quarantine health sheet,
# filled in fortnightly, by a man with a round of houses to get through before four.
#
