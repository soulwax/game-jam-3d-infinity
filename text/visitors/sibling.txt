# THE QUARANTINE — the sibling. Days 2, 5, 9, 10, 14, 19.
#
# Highest emotional stake in the game, and the load-bearing test of PLAN 11.1 rule 3:
# THE PLAYER MUST SOMETIMES BE RIGHT — and the sibling must sometimes simply be right
# too. Branches marked TRUE below are plainly, verifiably true. A player who corrects
# one of them is the one who has drifted. The sibling is gracious about it, and that
# is meant to sting more than being right would have.
#
# Boring explanation, held for all of days 2-14 and never confirmed: families
# remember the same afternoon three different ways, and this one always has.
