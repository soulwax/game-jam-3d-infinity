# THE QUARANTINE — the caller, Mrs Hallow. Days 5, 10, 20.
# She is at the wrong door only in the sense that the right person has gone. She asks
# after the previous tenant (PLAN §24.1) and never once says their name, and never
# notices she hasn't — she talks the way people talk about someone you both know.
# The player cannot ask a question that resolves it. She is withholding nothing.
# She is never frightening and she never escalates. The dread is structural: somebody
# keeps arriving to collect from a person who is in no record the player has.
# Branch encoding per the wave brief §2: full.N / compressed.N, one line per tier.

# ever explaining them. Boring explanation for all of it: she is owed a dish.
