# THE QUARANTINE — the warden. Days 5, 8, 13, 15, 20.
# The face of the quarantine: official, procedural, never a threat. The register is the
# broadcast's own passive, spoken aloud by someone who has said it forty times today.
# They carry a pen and the compliance record. They never gloat, press, or come back to it.
# Branch encoding per the wave brief: full.N / compressed.N, one line per tier.
