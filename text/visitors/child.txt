# THE QUARANTINE — the child. Days 3, 8, 14, 19.
#
# Sylvia, number 3. Every line is a voice at knee height through the letterbox: she is
# not allowed out, you are not allowed out, and the slot is the whole relationship.
#
# She is never uncanny, never sing-song, never in riddles, and she never knows a thing
# she has not seen out of her own front window. What she has instead is the only mouth
# on this street with no reason to be tactful. Adults are managing each other. She says
# the plain thing, asks the direct question, and reports what she saw with total
# confidence and a child's unreliable sense of when.
#
# Boring explanation, held for days 3-14 and never confirmed: a bored eight-year-old
# with a window and nobody her own age to talk to. Her house numbers are wrong the way
# children's house numbers are wrong.
#
# Branch encoding per the wave brief §2: full.N / compressed.N, contiguous from 1.
